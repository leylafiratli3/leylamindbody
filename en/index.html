About Me

Through my own journey of healing chronic back pain, anxiety and panic attacks I discovered that the only person that can heal us is ourselves. When we begin to listen to the message of our nervous systems, symptoms loosen their grip — and lasting freedom becomes possible.

I do this work to help people reconnect with that power, and to offer an alternative to the cycles of hospitals, medications and Western therapy that so often fail us.

I believe that with commitment, patience, and trust, anyone can be the source of their own healing. My role is simply to create the space, tools, and support for that transformation to unfold through breathwork, somatic awareness, and mind-body understanding.

The Foundation of My Work

My work is guided by these core principles that emerged from my own healing journey and my understanding of how our world impacts our bodies.

Your nervous system is reacting to an unhealthy world

Chronic pain, anxiety, overwhelm and disconnection are intelligent responses of your body trying to survive during end stage capitalism. There is nothing wrong or broken with you.

Healing comes from listening and understanding

Lasting freedom comes from listening and understanding the message of our nervous systems. When we listen to what our bodies are trying to tell us, the need for symptoms diminishes.

Empowerment over dependency

My role is not to make you reliant on me, but be a source of support as you heal yourself. I provide space, guidance, and practices so you can become your own healer. The more people who reclaim their agency, the more we loosen the grip of the systems. I hope after some time of working together, you will no longer need me.

Individual healing enables collective change

Your personal healing is an act of collective liberation. When we heal individually, we strengthen the collective. When the collective heals, we transform the systems that cause suffering. Your healing matters for the world.

Rooted in love and empathy

I approach this work with deep love and empathy for all human beings. I meet you where you are without judgment, with the understanding that we are all doing our best in an imperfect system. Your humanity is honored in every session.

These principles shape every session, every conversation, and every practice I offer.

Each time we reconnect with our body, the hold of systems that thrive on our disconnection weakens. I share this work to contribute to the wider liberation that becomes possible when we are no longer bound by pain.
