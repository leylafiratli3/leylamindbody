Work with me

Breathwork and somatic work

This is a space to honor your body's innate wisdom and capacity for freedom. Personalized 1:1 breathwork and somatic sessions help you connect deeply with yourself, create space around all that's moving through you, and cultivate a sense of embodied agency.

Book a free intro call

My approach

Agency-centered & personalized

Every session is tailored to you, honoring your needs, experiences, and intentions. You are the expert of your own body, and my role is to support you in listening to its wisdom. We move entirely at your pace, no rush, no judgment, only gentle guidance as you explore what arises.

Trauma-informed

Safety is the foundation of all our work. I create a container where you feel held, respected, and empowered to set boundaries and explore at your own comfort level. Your nervous system guides the journey, and we honor whatever emerges with compassion and curiosity.

Body-led

Your body holds the story of your experiences, and it also holds the key to your freedom. I trust the profound wisdom of your somatic intelligence: your body's natural ability to regulate, release, and restore balance.

Breath as bridge

Your breath is the bridge between conscious awareness and your autonomic nervous system. I work with a range of breath patterns, and find conscious connected breathwork a beautiful way to walk across that bridge together.

What to expect

Somatic awareness

We gently tune into the subtle sensations in your body, the places where you hold tension, the areas that feel alive with energy, and the spaces that feel like nothing. Through mindful awareness, you learn to listen to your body's wisdom and respond with kindness.

Breath connection

Your breath is a powerful tool for change. Together we explore breathing techniques that help you:

  • Release stored stress and tension
  • Access deeper states of relaxation and clarity
  • Process emotions and experiences safely
  • Cultivate presence and embodiment
  • Strengthen your mind-body connection

Every session includes breathwork and somatic awareness; the specific techniques evolve based on what you need.

Who this is for

These sessions are for you if you're ready to:

  • Create awareness and space around intergenerational patterns and ancestral trauma that show up as chronic tension, anxiety, or emotional dysregulation in your body
  • Address the somatic impacts of oppression, colonial violence, and systemic injustice that live in your nervous system and tissues
  • Reclaim your embodied sovereignty by learning to listen to your body's wisdom and make choices from a place of authentic safety
  • Develop somatic tools for navigating triggers, emotional overwhelm, and stress responses rooted in trauma
  • Cultivate nervous system resilience, so you can stay present and regulated even in challenging moments
  • Create space around stored experiences that no longer serve your growth
  • Strengthen your capacity for self-regulation and co-regulation in relationships and community

The process

Initial connection

We begin with a conversation to understand your goals, intentions, and the areas you'd like to explore, so I can tailor our sessions to you.

Session structure

Each session typically includes grounding and centering to establish safety, guided exploration of body sensations, breath (conscious connected breath or another style, based on what you need), integration and reflection, and practical tools to use between sessions.

Ongoing support

I offer guidance between sessions, helping you weave the work into your daily life.
