Often, yes. The nervous system plays a big part in both migraines and tension headaches, and many people notice that theirs follow stress or other triggers.

## Where the nervous system comes in

Migraine is a recognized neurological condition, and stress and the state of your nervous system are among its best-known triggers. Tension headaches are tied even more directly to how much the body is bracing: the jaw clenched, the shoulders up around the ears, the forehead tight.

Mind-body work doesn't replace your medical care. It works with the part your nervous system plays, and for most people, that part is very big.

## How do I know if my headaches might be mind-body?

Some signs I look for:

- They come at predictable times, like on weekends, or right after a deadline passes.
- They started during a hard period of your life.
- You have other symptoms that come and go, like back pain or gut trouble.
- Your tests are normal.

None of these proves anything on its own. But the more you recognize, the more likely your nervous system is involved. I wrote more in [How do I know if my pain might be mind-body?](/en/writing/how-do-i-know-if-my-pain-is-mind-body.html)

## When should a headache be checked right away?

Go to an emergency room if you have:

- a sudden, severe headache, the worst you've ever had
- a headache with weakness, confusion, loss of vision or trouble speaking
- a headache with a fever and a stiff neck
- a headache after a blow to the head

## Living by a list of triggers

Many people with migraines live by a long list of things to avoid: certain foods, bright light, the weather, sleeping too little or too much. The list keeps growing, and life gets smaller around it.

I think the fear of the next migraine can keep the nervous system on alert all by itself. Part of what we do is work with that fear, slowly, so your world can get bigger again.

## Related questions

### Should I stop taking my migraine medication?

That's a decision for you and your doctor. This work sits alongside medical care, not in place of it.

### Does mind-body mean my headaches aren't real?

No. The pain is real and physical. Mind-body is about where it comes from.

### Why do I get migraines on weekends?

Many people get headaches when stress suddenly drops, like on a weekend or the first day of a holiday. The nervous system has been holding on for so long that the letdown itself becomes a trigger.

### Can stress cause tension headaches?

Yes. Stress makes the muscles of the jaw, neck and scalp tighten, often without us noticing. Over time, that constant bracing can turn into regular headaches.
